\section{Rudy Schmitz}

\subsection{Did we split up tasks in a fair manner, or jointly work through the exercises?}
In the first week we lost a team member. After some investigation we set ourselves a challenging but achievable goal in the few weeks before the deadline. We divided part of the project into 4 chunks (real bios, synthetic bios, filter of synthetic bios, training in bios).
This is a fair division, but not a very wise one. The dependencies of the chunks and our different schedules (different classes) caused some delays and friction. But then a norming, storming, performing cycle is normal for every team. So, to make the deadline we adopted an agile approach. Nobody needed to be given a task, they just got done.

\subsection{Did every group member contribute equally?}
Not all group members contributed equally, because we did different tasks. We contributed to the best of our ability, with passion, ownership and a shared goal. Did every group member contribute enough? More than enough. I appreciate that, because I know it will have cost them time to prepare for their exams.

\subsection{Do some members of our group deserve a different grade from others?}
Yes. I think they all deserve it, so divide any bonus there is among them. I already had the bonus of working with them.
